1. Privacy Overview

At Visual Risk and AI Intelligence System, we prioritize the confidentiality and protection of your personal digital twin data. This Privacy Policy explains how your financial, academic, fitness, and lifestyle logs are collected, processed, stored, and utilized within our digital twin framework.

By registering an account and using our personal decision-support system, you agree to the data collection and processing methods described in this document.

2. Information We Collect

To construct your digital twin model and perform predictive future simulations, we collect the following types of information:

Account & Profile
  • Username, Email Address, Hashed Password
  • Age, Occupation
  • Baseline Monthly Income & Expenses
  • Baseline Sleep and Fitness targets
Financial Data
  • Income and Expense Transaction logs
  • Category breakdowns & timestamps
  • Accumulated Savings balances
Academic Logs
  • Study hours & subject logs
  • Self-rated productivity scores (%)
  • Predicted GPA indicators
Fitness & Lifestyle
  • Activity types, durations, and calorie logs
  • Personal Goals and target achievement dates
  • Chat interactions with the AI Assistant

3. How We Use Your Data

Your logged metrics are used strictly to provide you with personal intelligence and decision simulation features:

  • Digital Twin Modeling: Building a real-time behavioral model of your lifestyle habits.
  • Predictive Analytics: Executing Scikit-Learn linear regression models to project 6-month savings forecasts, GPA trajectories, and 30-day activity trends.
  • Decision Simulation: Calculating risk scores and outcome impacts for "What-if" scenarios (Saving, Investing, Spending, Studying, and Exercising).
  • Personalized Coaching: Contextualizing conversational prompts for Google Gemini AI to deliver tailored recommendations.

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

Our system integrates predictive machine learning frameworks and conversational generative AI:

  • Google Gemini AI Integration: When you converse with the AI Assistant, a contextual summary of your profile (e.g., income, savings rate, study hours, active goals) is sent via encrypted API to Google Gemini to generate personalized advice.
  • Local Machine Learning: Linear regression algorithms for forecasting (GPA, savings, fitness trends) are computed locally on our Flask server environment using scikit-learn and NumPy.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or conversation logs to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ standard security protocols to safeguard your credentials and activity logs:

Password Security: Passwords are hashed using Werkzeug's secure password hashing algorithms before storage.

Session Management: User sessions are securely managed via standard Flask-Login authentication.

Role-Based Access: Administrative users have access to platform metrics and management tools through secured admin pathways.

6. Your Data Rights & Controls

You maintain full ownership over your digital twin data:

  • View & Access: View all logged transactions, study sessions, and fitness logs through your personal dashboard.
  • Edit & Delete: Update or delete individual transaction records, study entries, and fitness logs at any time.
  • Simulation History: Clear your simulation engine history with one click.
